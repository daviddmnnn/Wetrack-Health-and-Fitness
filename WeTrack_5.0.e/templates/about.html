WETRACK 3.0

Individual Health & Fitness Data Tracking System

WeTrack is a prototype for storing and managing client information and health and fitness records, with predictive and progress-tracking features.

PURPOSE

What WeTrack does

The system brings personal information, fitness logs, diet records, sleep records, goals and exercise plans into one tracking environment. Health Prediction uses recorded data to produce simple record-based projections.
