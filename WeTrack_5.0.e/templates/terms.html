Terms & Conditions

Please read how WeTrack uses your information before creating an account.

1. About WeTrack

WeTrack is a school project and research prototype for recording personal health and fitness information. It is intended for personal tracking and demonstration purposes. It is not a medical device or a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

2. Information collected

To create and operate your account, the system stores your name, birthdate, gender, email address, mobile number, username, and a password hash. If you choose to add one, it also stores your profile photo.

When you use the system, it stores the health measurements and records you provide, including height, weight, blood type, physical activities, steps, meals, sleep, goals, exercise-plan progress, and reports or feedback.

3. How information is used

Your information is used to authenticate you, show your profile and history, calculate BMI and record summaries, display charts, track goals, and produce educational estimates from your records. Reports and feedback are stored so they can be reviewed by the project team.

Keep your account credentials private. Do not enter information you do not want stored in this project system.

4. Predictions and health information

Predictions and suggestions are simple estimates based only on the information entered. Records may be incomplete or inaccurate. Do not use the estimates to make medical decisions; consult a qualified health professional for health concerns.

5. Your choices and rights

You can review and update eligible profile details, change your username or password, and delete your account from Security Settings. Deleting your account removes the account and its associated records from the system. You may choose not to provide optional profile information, though some features may then have less data to display.

6. Changes and acceptance

These terms may be updated as the school project changes. The current version will be available on this page. By selecting the acceptance box during registration and creating an account, you confirm that you have read and agree to these terms.
